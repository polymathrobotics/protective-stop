# 6-Hour Fleet Soak — Final Verification (2026-08-03)

**Verdict: PASS.** Over 6 h 11 m, all reachable units ran the same clean committed
build with **zero reboots, zero crashes, zero safety faults, zero heap leaks, and
zero unreachability**. The single logged anomaly was one transient control-plane
reconnect during startup settling, self-healed in seconds.

## Setup

- **Firmware:** all units OTA-updated to `95a948b` (a clean, committed, non-dirty
  build — replacing a prior mix of `f4daf48-dirty` / `1cfbd9a` / `3dcd08d`).
  `95a948b` also adds the `ml_reconnects` telemetry field (see below).
- **Units under test** (the reachable fleet; 6 other units were offline):

  | Unit | ID | Role in soak | Path |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | machn-01d77a1c | `0x01d77a1c` | machine (drives 2 series relays) | Ethernet |
  | PSTOP54 | `0x01d7eed0` | remote — **live safety link to machn** | USB-NCM |
  | PSTOP42 | `0x01d7ed38` | remote — control-plane/network soak | USB-NCM |
  | DUT | `0x01d7f344` | remote — **DERP-relayed** control-plane soak | DERP relay |

- **Window:** 2026-08-03 00:43:37 → 06:54:10 (6 h 11 m monitored; units up
  ~6 h 15 m continuous). Poll interval 30 s → **716 samples/unit, 2864 total.**
  (Monitoring ran in two windows — the host killed the first background task at
  4 h 44 m — but the units ran continuously the whole time and self-track cumulative
  counters, so coverage is complete. A ~5 min fine-grained polling gap at 05:27 has
  no reboot/fault in the surrounding cumulative counters.)
- **Machine state:** fail-safe STOP (unarmed) throughout — the safety *link* is
  soaked (heartbeats + status streaming + replies), not the energized-RUN path
  (which would need a continuously-driven arming gesture).

- **No restarts:** every unit's uptime climbed monotonically to ~6 h 15 m;
  `reset_reason` was `3` (SW — the initial OTA boot) for all, with no fault code.
- **Heap stable:** machn 8019k→8020k; PSTOP54 7917k→7918k (min dip 7852k, recovered);
  PSTOP42 7918k→7921k; DUT 7917k→7920k. No downward trend on any unit.
- **DERP path held:** the DERP-relayed DUT stayed `ml_state=CONNECTED` for all 716
  samples — historically the flakiest path, solid here.

### The live safety link (PSTOP54 ↔ machn)

- **`bonded=1` on every one of 716 machn samples** — the bond never dropped.
- **110,817 status messages sent, 110,687 replies — a 99.88 % reply rate over 6 h.**
- Rebonds: 13 → 32 (**+19 total**). ~10 of those were a single settling burst in the
  first 14 min (right after four near-simultaneous OTA reboots); the remaining ~9
  were sporadic over 6 h (≈ 1 per 40 min), each a brief self-healing re-bond that
  never caused a STOP, a mismatch, or a lost bond. `pstop_send_fail` 11 → 20 (+9).
- `wg_dedup_drops` climbed on the USB-NCM units (harmless — dropping duplicate
  WireGuard packets that arrive via both direct and relay paths).

### The one logged anomaly

`00:49:17 machn: ml_state=3 (RECONNECTING)` — a single transient during startup
settling, recovered within seconds; it is one of machn's 2 `ml_reconnects`. Both
machn reconnects landed by 00:57; **zero reconnects in the following ~6 h.**

## Notes

- **`ml_reconnects` (new this build) earned its place:** it caught machn's 2
  control-plane reconnects, which self-recovered between 30 s polls and would
  otherwise have been invisible. `0` on all three remotes = the control plane never
  flapped for them.
- **`rst_hist` historical entries are pre-soak:** PSTOP42's ring buffer contains
  older `6` (TASK_WDT) entries and PSTOP54 older `11` (USB) entries — these predate
  this firmware and this soak (leftover from bench flashing / USB re-enumeration).
  No unit incurred a watchdog or fault reset *during* the 6 h.
- **Monitor correction:** an early false-positive ("machn link stale") came from
  applying remote-role semantics to the machine (`pstop_last_reply_ms` is an
  absolute boot-timestamp, and a machine sends rather than receives replies). The
  check was switched to a reply-counter-progress test; the device was never stale.

## Assessment

The fleet is stable for extended continuous operation on `95a948b`. No restarts,
no disconnects at the bond level, no safety faults, no resource leaks. The only
imperfection is a small rate of brief, self-healing re-bonds on the one active
wireless-tunnelled safety link (~1 per 40 min, 99.88 % reply rate) — worth keeping
an eye on but well within normal for a WireGuard/DERP-tunnelled link and never
consequential to the safety function.
